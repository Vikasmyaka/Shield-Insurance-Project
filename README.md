# Shield-Insurance-Project
Shield Insurance Analytics Dashboard
Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI Insurance Analytics Dashboard developed as part of an internship project. The dashboard helps analyze customer growth, revenue performance, city-wise business trends, sales channel effectiveness, age-group segmentation, and monthly seasonal patterns.

The main objective of this project is to transform raw insurance business data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making.

Dashboard Insights
1. General Business Analysis
Customer base increased by more than 18% month-over-month.
Revenue reached nearly ₹989M.
March 2023 recorded peak customer activity.
April showed a slight correction after strong growth.
Business Impact

Helps management monitor overall company growth and identify seasonal demand patterns.

4. Age Group Analysis
Key Findings
Age 31–40 is the largest customer segment.
Senior customers (65+) generate high premium value.
Young customers (18–24) contribute lower revenue.
Business Impact

Enables personalized insurance offerings and targeted marketing campaigns.

5. Monthly Trends & Seasonality
Trend Observations
Stable customer activity between Nov–Feb.
Massive customer spike observed in March 2023.
April showed post-peak normalization.
Business Impact

Helps forecast demand and identify seasonal business behavior.

Business Recommendations
Increase investment in digital acquisition channels.
Focus retention campaigns on the 31–40 age group.
Create premium insurance plans for senior customers.
Expand marketing efforts in emerging cities.
Use predictive analytics for demand forecasting.
